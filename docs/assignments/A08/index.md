# A8: Gear Box

In this project we were tasked with parametrically designing and selecting parts for a gear assembly to meet given specifications. Our assemblies consist of two gears (input and output), with gear ratio 24:9. The output gear is to have 1.25 lbf-in torque and 1100 rpm speed. The shaft diameters are .125in and .25in respectively, with both having a length of 1 inch. We are to assume the gear train is ideal and the moment of the radial force from the gear train is negligiable.

# Input and Output Shafts: 
To confirm that our given input shaft diameter of .125in was sufficient, I determined the input torque using the given output torque and gear ratio, then used the max stress formula to find the minimum acceptable diameter value. After finding an acceptable value of .0842 in, I repeated the same process for the output shaft and once again found a value below the given diameter.

# Bearing Material:
I began by listing my known values, then setting up equations for Ft, Fr, P, V, and PV. Next I plugged in values for both my input and output gears. Finally, after referencing the material list from the lecture slides, I decided on graphite as it was within the calculated tolerance for all parameters.
